Care plan preset

Pick the doctor's recommended plan to pre-fill the phases and x-ray timing below. Insurance, bolt-ons, and SAVINGS are still entered manually for every plan. Switching presets overwrites the phases/x-ray sections — any manual edits made below will be replaced.

Patient

Skipped weeks don't change the care plan itself (visit count, re-exams, x-rays, cost) — they only push the calendar dates out for each week skipped, since that week won't have any visits. Multiple skips stack correctly, whether they're spread across the plan or consecutive (use "how many weeks" for a multi-week closure in one place). A footnote naming the closed week(s) prints under the schedule table.

Care plan phases

Add a row per frequency phase. Each phase has its own re-exam interval (resets per phase). The last visit of your final phase always doubles as the anniversary exam (billed as a re-exam), and one final report visit — adjustment only — is auto-added right after it. Adjustment is always billed alongside any re-exam/x-ray day; these are never stand-alone visits.

X-rays

X-rays are tied to WHICH re-exam they happen at — counted across the whole plan in order (re-exam #1, #2, #3...), not to the end of a specific phase. E.g. "x-ray at re-exam #4" fires whenever the 4th re-exam of the plan lands, regardless of which phase it's in.

Insurance

Coinsurance % is what the PATIENT pays (insurance covers the rest). Deductible is one running balance shared across adjustments, re-exams, x-rays, TX Phase, and any insurance-billed bolt-ons. Priority alternates month by month: within each month, the core plan (adjustments/re-exams/x-rays/TX Phase) claims the deductible first, then that same month's bolt-ons draw on whatever's left, then the next month's core gets first crack at whatever remains, and so on. Once the visit cap is hit, adjustments, re-exams, AND x-rays all switch to self-pay together — insurance is treated as exhausted for the rest of the plan.

Bolt-on services

For each service, set how many times a week it happens, which week it starts, and for how many weeks (leave blank to run through the end of the plan). Add a second frequency if the pace changes partway through. Occurrences land on the first N visits of each applicable week, from your care plan schedule.

Click the plan that applies. Percentages are what the patient is responsible for (not the discount). Savings only apply to self-pay charges — never to anything insurance is covering.
